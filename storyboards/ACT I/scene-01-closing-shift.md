# Scene 01 - Closing Shift at Marlowe's Market

## Scene Purpose

This scene introduces `Legion` from ground level. The player should not know what Legion is, where it came from, or what it wants. The opening should feel ordinary first, then quietly wrong, then violently interrupted.

The player begins as a normal person at work in the fictional town of `Briarwake`. The scene establishes routine, place, character, and tone before the crash changes everything.

## Opening Recommendation

Start with the main character at work. Do not open in space.

The space origin of Legion should be saved for later visions, corrupted broadcasts, dreams, or recovered memories. The first scene is stronger if the player discovers the event with the protagonist instead of watching an explanation before play begins.

## First Image

Black screen.

Faint fluorescent hum.

A register scanner beeps once.

Fade in to a compact 16-bit style grocery and convenience store interior. It is late. The aisles are mostly empty. The front windows show a dark parking lot, a few weak streetlights, and the tree line beyond `Ashbell Road`.

Small text appears briefly:

```text
11:43 PM
Marlowe's Market
Briarwake
```

No narration explains the town. The player sees it through details.

## Player Control

The player gets control after a short line from the manager or coworker.

Suggested opening line:

```text
HOLLIS:
If that freezer starts screaming again, kick the panel twice.
Once makes it worse.
```

The line should be mundane, slightly funny, and local. It immediately says: this is a normal place with normal problems.

## Main Character State

The main character is near the end of a late shift. They are tired but functional. They know the store, the people, and the town. They are not expecting anything important to happen tonight.

Suggested character assumptions for now:

- Works closing shifts at `Marlowe's Market`.
- Lives in or near Briarwake.
- Knows local customers by face, even if not by name.
- Has a personal reason to want the shift to end.
- Is observant enough to notice things others dismiss.

## Playable Goals

The scene should begin with small work tasks. These are not busywork only; they teach interaction and reveal character.

### Required Tasks

- Check the front counter.
- Speak to the coworker or manager.
- Inspect the radio.
- Handle one last customer.
- Walk near the front windows before the crash.

### Optional Tasks

- Restock a shelf.
- Check the humming freezer.
- Read the community board.
- Take out trash near the side door.
- Inspect a family photo, old receipt, or personal item near the employee area.
- Try to call or message someone.

## Map Layout

### Front Counter

The register, radio, gum rack, scratch cards from the fictional `Kestrel Draw`, and a small bell that customers never use.

### Aisles

Short, readable aisles with snacks, canned food, batteries, cheap toys, and basic medicine.

### Freezer Wall

A noisy freezer that flickers and rattles. This becomes a useful source of misdirection before the crash.

### Back Room

Employee lockers, mop sink, old posters, stacked boxes, and a delivery door.

### Front Windows

The main visual connection to the outside. Before the crash, the player can see the parking lot, `Ashbell Road`, and the distant line of `Blackneedle Woods`.

### Side Door

Optional route for taking out trash. It gives an early glimpse of the night sky and the radio tower lights.

## Characters Present

### Player Character

Temporary name until finalized.

Role in scene:

- Performs closing tasks.
- Talks to locals.
- Notices small wrong details.
- Becomes the player's point of view into the crash.

### Hollis Marlowe

Owner or night manager of `Marlowe's Market`.

Personality:

- Dry, tired, practical.
- Knows every local rumor but pretends not to care.
- Treats the player character like someone reliable.

Scene role:

- Gives the first task.
- Grounds the store as a real workplace.
- May become endangered after the crash.

### Mara Vale

Recommended first party member, introduced here as coworker.

Personality:

- Direct.
- Capable under pressure.
- More protective than she admits.
- Knows side routes through Briarwake.

Scene role:

- Handles closing work with the player.
- Comments on strange town details.
- Can become the first recruit after the crash.

### Silas Kade

Last customer before the crash.

Personality:

- Nervous local maintenance worker.
- Claims the old rail yard lights turned on by themselves.
- Buys batteries, canned coffee, and bandages.

Scene role:

- Foreshadows trouble near `Calder Yard`.
- May be seen later infected or injured.

### Unnamed Customer

A quiet person by the front window.

Personality:

- Polite but distracted.
- Hears something others do not.

Scene role:

- Shows the first subtle infection-like behavior before the crash.
- Should not become an enemy yet.

## Environmental Foreshadowing

These details should be interactive or visible during the shift.

### Radio Interference

The counter radio plays a local night show from fictional station `KBRW`.

Possible lines:

```text
KBRW HOST:
...and if you're seeing lights over Blackneedle tonight, no, we don't know either.
County dispatch says it's probably weather equipment from Rook Signal.
```

Then static cuts in. For a second, two voices overlap.

```text
RADIO:
alone alone alone
```

The radio snaps back to normal. No one else reacts strongly.

### The Freezer

The freezer hums at a pitch that almost sounds like a voice. Hollis dismisses it as an old compressor.

If inspected:

```text
The freezer vibrates through the floor.
For a second, your teeth ache.
```

### The Flies

A few flies gather near the window despite the cold. They move in a tight cluster, then scatter when approached.

If inspected:

```text
The flies crawl across the glass in the same direction.
Toward the woods.
```

### The Community Board

Fictional local notices:

- `Foundry Row Block Sale - Saturday`
- `Missing: Orange Cat named Pipp`
- `Rook Signal Maintenance Road Closed`
- `Vesper Clinic Blood Drive`
- `Briarwake Harvest Parade Volunteers Needed`

These establish town life and future locations without using real places.

### Customer Repetition

The unnamed customer says the same thing twice in slightly different emotional tones.

First:

```text
CUSTOMER:
Long night.
```

Later:

```text
CUSTOMER:
Long night.
Long, long night.
```

## Full Scene Contract

### Scene Start

The game begins inside `Marlowe's Market` at `11:43 PM`.

The player does not see the crash, the alien organism, space, the military, a scientist, or any explanation. The scene is built around ordinary routine becoming impossible.

### Scene End

The scene ends when the player exits through the front door after the crash and steps onto `Ashbell Road`.

### Required Outcome

By the end of the scene:

- The player understands the protagonist works at `Marlowe's Market`.
- The player has met `Hollis Marlowe`, `Mara Vale`, `Silas Kade`, and the quiet window customer.
- The player has learned basic movement and interaction.
- The player has completed a small set of closing-shift tasks.
- The player has seen at least three strange details before the crash.
- The crash has happened.
- The player has found or been offered a flashlight.
- The quiet customer has spoken one impossible line and left the store.
- The player has a clear objective to investigate the glow beyond `Blackneedle Woods`.

### Scene Length Target

First playthrough: 8 to 14 minutes.

Repeat playthrough, skipping optional interactions: 4 to 6 minutes.

### Player Mood Target

The player should feel:

1. Comfortable enough to explore.
2. Slightly uneasy.
3. Interrupted by something too large to understand.
4. Responsible for checking on nearby people.
5. Pulled outside by curiosity and concern.

## Camera, Audio, And Presentation

### Opening Audio

The first sound is not music. It is store ambience:

- fluorescent light buzz
- freezer compressor rattle
- faint register hum
- soft radio static
- distant tires on wet road outside
- a single register scanner beep

After two seconds, a quiet track fades in. It should be warm, small, and repetitive, like a tired end-of-night routine. The music should have one faint detuned note that feels accidental.

### Opening Visual

Black screen.

Scanner beep.

Fade in from black to the front counter of `Marlowe's Market`.

The camera is top-down or slightly angled top-down in a compact 16-bit RPG style. The player character stands behind or near the counter. Hollis is at the register. Mara is visible down an aisle. The quiet customer stands near the front windows.

Small location card:

```text
11:43 PM
Marlowe's Market
Briarwake
```

The location card fades without input.

### Lighting Before The Crash

- Warm light over the counter.
- Colder light in the aisles.
- Freezer wall flickers slightly.
- Front windows show dark blue-black outside.
- The far tree line of `Blackneedle Woods` is visible as a jagged silhouette.
- `Rook Signal Tower` blinks faintly in the distance if the player looks through the side door or front window.

### Lighting After The Crash

- Main lights fail.
- Emergency strips create red-orange pools of light.
- Freezer glow becomes the strongest interior light.
- A pale green-white glow pulses from the windows.
- Small dust particles drift in the light shafts.
- Broken glass near the front window catches the glow.

`Marlowe's Market` should be a small rectangular map, large enough to explore but small enough that the player cannot get lost.

Suggested zones:

- `Front Counter`
- `Aisle One: Snacks And Drinks`
- `Aisle Two: Household And Medicine`
- `Freezer Wall`
- `Community Board`
- `Front Windows`
- `Employee Hall`
- `Back Room`
- `Side Door`
- `Parking Lot View`

### Front Counter

Contains:

- register
- counter radio
- receipt printer
- lottery-style tickets from fictional `Kestrel Draw`
- gum rack
- taped note from Hollis
- plastic cup with pens
- small bell nobody uses

Interaction purpose:

- introduces the radio
- allows conversation with Hollis
- teaches inspecting objects

### Aisle One: Snacks And Drinks

Contains:

- chip bags
- bottled sodas

### Freezer Wall

Contains:

Interaction purpose:

- If the player helps Hollis first after the crash, Hollis trusts them more in later scenes.
- If the player helps Mara first after the crash, Mara joins with a stronger initial bond.

## Main Scene Flow

### Phase 1: Fade-In And First Line

The player cannot move for the first few seconds.

Sequence:

1. Black screen.
2. Scanner beep.
3. Fade in to front counter.
4. Radio is quietly playing.
5. Hollis leans over the register, tapping a stuck key.
6. Mara crosses the top of the screen carrying a small box.
7. Quiet customer stands near the window, facing outside.
8. Location card appears and fades.
9. Hollis speaks.

Opening dialogue:

```text
HOLLIS:
If that freezer starts screaming again, kick the panel twice.
Once makes it worse.

MARA:
That is not how repairs work.

HOLLIS:
It is how this store works.
```

Control returns.

First objective appears:

```text
Finish closing tasks.
```

### Phase 2: Closing Task List Unlocks

When the player opens the menu or talks to Hollis again, the current closing list is visible.

Initial task list:

```text
Closing Tasks
- Check the counter radio
- Restock Aisle Two
- Speak with Mara
- Help the last customer
- Check the front windows
```

Only `Check the counter radio`, `Restock Aisle Two`, and `Speak with Mara` are immediately available. `Help the last customer` triggers after two tasks. `Check the front windows` becomes required after Silas leaves.

### Phase 3: Free Exploration Before Silas

The player can walk around the store and inspect objects.

Allowed interactions:

- Hollis
- Mara
- radio
- freezer
- community board
- front windows
- back room
- employee schedule
- personal item
- side door
- trash bin
- quiet customer

The scene should not rush the player. The store is safe at this point.

### Phase 4: First Strange Detail Trigger

After the player completes any two of these actions:

- speaks to Mara
- inspects radio
- restocks shelf
- checks freezer
- reads community board
- takes trash out

The radio glitches for the first time.

Radio moment:

```text
KBRW HOST:
...still no confirmation from county dispatch on the lights over Blackneedle.
If you are calling from Foundry Row, stay on the line, I can barely--

RADIO:
alone alone alone

KBRW HOST:
--hear you. Huh. Lost the board for a second there.
```

Mara reacts only if she is nearby:

```text
MARA:
Did he just say "alone"?

HOLLIS:
Late-night radio. They say worse after midnight.
```

### Phase 5: Silas Enters

The front door chime plays. `Silas Kade` enters quickly, as if he has been walking fast.

He should not look infected. He looks frightened and sleep-deprived.

Silas movement:

1. Enters front door.
2. Pauses near quiet customer.
3. Looks out window toward `Blackneedle Woods`.
4. Walks to Aisle Two.
5. Picks up batteries and bandages.
6. Walks to Aisle One.
7. Picks up canned coffee.
8. Goes to counter.

If the player approaches before he reaches the counter:

```text
SILAS:
Evening.

PLAYER CHARACTER:
You good?

SILAS:
That's a large question for this late.
```

At the counter, the player must interact with the register to serve him.

Silas checkout dialogue:

```text
PLAYER CHARACTER:
Working late?

SILAS:
Was.

HOLLIS:
Something wrong out by the yard?

SILAS:
You ever seen rail lights turn on without power?

MARA:
In Briarwake? Sure. Right before the bill arrives.

SILAS:
No. I mean all of them. One after another.
Like something was walking down the line.

PLAYER CHARACTER:
Maybe Rook Signal is testing again.

SILAS:
Rook blinks red. This was under the ground.
```

After checkout:

```text
SILAS:
If anyone asks, I was never there tonight.

HOLLIS:
Silas, you paid with exact change and signed the receipt.

SILAS:
Then tell them I was less there than usual.
```

Silas exits through the front door.

### Phase 6: Window Pull

After Silas leaves, the quiet customer remains by the window.

Mara notices the customer is not moving.

```text
MARA:
How long has that one been standing there?
```

The player must approach the front windows or quiet customer.

If the player talks to the customer:

```text
PLAYER CHARACTER:
Store closes soon.

QUIET CUSTOMER:
I know.

PLAYER CHARACTER:
You waiting for someone?

QUIET CUSTOMER:
I think so.
```

If the player inspects the window instead:

```text
You look past Ashbell Road.
The tree line flickers.
Not lightning.
Too low.
Too steady.
```

When this inspection finishes, set `scene01_checked_window`.

### Phase 7: Final Normal Moment

The store appears to return to routine for a few seconds.

Hollis speaks from the counter:

```text
HOLLIS:
All right. Lock the front after our statue decides to buy something.
Then everyone go home before Briarwake invents another problem.
```

Mara moves toward the back room.

Quiet customer turns their head slightly toward the tree line.

The music drops to only two notes.

### Phase 8: Pressure Drop

The player loses control.

Sequence:

1. All ambient sound lowers suddenly.
2. The freezer hum becomes louder.
3. The radio cuts to static.
4. The front door chime rings once even though nobody enters.
5. The quiet customer whispers something too low to read.
6. The player's sprite takes one involuntary step toward the window.
7. The lights dim.
8. The radio emits one clear tone.

Displayed text:

```text
For one second, everything in the store listens.
```

### Phase 9: The Crash

The crash must feel enormous but still mysterious.

Crash sequence:

1. Shelves vibrate.
2. Bottled drinks rattle in a wave from back to front.
3. The counter radio turns itself up.
4. The register drawer snaps open.
5. A white-green flash fills the windows.
6. The screen shakes once, hard.
7. A layered sound hits: pressure, metal tearing, thunder, and a voice-like burst.
8. The front window cracks but does not fully break.
9. Main lights fail.
10. Something heavy falls in the back room.
11. A shelf in Aisle One spills items.
12. The quiet customer remains standing.

During the sound burst, the radio briefly displays impossible overlapping text:

```text
RADIO:
we are we are we are
```

This should happen quickly enough that the player may not fully process it.

### Phase 10: Immediate Aftermath

The store is dark.

No one speaks for two seconds.

First line:

```text
HOLLIS:
Everybody still got the same number of limbs?
```

Mara answers from near the back room:

```text
MARA:
I am going to pretend that helped.
```

New objective:

```text
Check on everyone.
```

The player regains control.

### Phase 11: Post-Crash Safety Check

Required checks:

- Check on Hollis.
- Check on Mara.
- Check the quiet customer.
- Find a flashlight.

These can be completed in any order except the quiet customer leaves after being checked and the flashlight must be found before exiting.

### Phase 12: Quiet Customer Leaves

When the player interacts with the quiet customer after the crash:

```text
PLAYER CHARACTER:
Are you hurt?

QUIET CUSTOMER:
No.

PLAYER CHARACTER:
You sure?

QUIET CUSTOMER:
It missed the ground.

PLAYER CHARACTER:
What did?

QUIET CUSTOMER:
The first part.
```

Mara, if nearby:

```text
MARA:
What does that mean?
```

The quiet customer walks to the door.

Hollis:

```text
HOLLIS:
Hey. Door's staying shut until we know what fell.
```

The quiet customer stops, then says:

```text
QUIET CUSTOMER:
It did not fall.
```

The quiet customer exits.

Set `scene01_customer_left`.

### Phase 13: Exterior Reveal From Inside

After the quiet customer leaves, the player can inspect the front windows.

Window text after crash:

```text
Ashbell Road is filling with porch lights and silhouettes.
Beyond them, Blackneedle Woods glows from below.
The light pulses like something breathing under the trees.
```

Mara:

```text
MARA:
That is past Harrowgate.
Maybe farther.
```

Hollis:

```text
HOLLIS:
No one goes toward the giant sky wound.
That is how people become cautionary plaques.
```

Player response options:

```text
- Someone could be hurt.
- We should at least see what happened.
- We call first.
```

All options lead to trying the phone or radio before leaving, but they establish tone.

### Phase 14: Failed Contact

The player must try one communication device:

- counter phone
- radio
- personal phone

Counter phone:

```text
The line clicks open.
No dial tone.
Under the static, something breathes in time with the freezer.
```

Radio:

```text
KBRW is gone.
Another signal sits under the static, too organized to be noise.
```

Personal phone:

```text
No service.
For one second, the screen shows one bar.
Then it disappears.
```

After one failed contact attempt, Hollis stops arguing as strongly.

```text
HOLLIS:
Fine. Look from the lot. Not the woods.
You see fire, you come back.
You see anything with too many opinions, you definitely come back.
```

### Phase 15: Exit The Store

To exit, the player must have:

- checked on Hollis
- checked on Mara
- checked the quiet customer
- found flashlight
- attempted contact

When the player opens the front door:

```text
The door sticks against broken glass.
Cold air pushes into the store.
Outside, Briarwake is awake.
```

Scene transition begins.

Final objective appears:

```text
Find out what crashed beyond Blackneedle.
```

The scene ends on the threshold, before the player gets full control outside.

## Detailed Task Design

### Task: Check The Counter Radio

Availability: Start of scene.

Location: Front counter.

Interaction before first glitch:

```text
The radio is tuned to KBRW, Briarwake's only station that still takes caller requests after ten.
```

If interacted with again:

```text
KBRW HOST:
Tonight's question: what is the worst road in Kestrel County and why is it Ashbell?
```

After first glitch:

```text
The tuning needle trembles even though nobody is touching it.
```

Completion:

- sets `scene01_inspected_radio`
- counts toward first strange detail trigger

### Task: Restock Aisle Two

Availability: Start of scene.

Location: Aisle Two.

Player picks up a small box from near the counter or back room and places it on a marked shelf.

Interaction:

```text
You shelve bandages, batteries, and cheap flashlights.
The kind of things people buy right before they pretend they are not worried.
```

Mara comment if nearby:

```text
MARA:
Put the good batteries in front.
Silas always digs through the whole row like one of them betrayed him.
```

Completion:

- sets `scene01_restocked_shelf`
- makes Silas's purchases feel connected to the world

### Task: Speak With Mara

Availability: Start of scene.

Location: Aisle One, then Aisle Two.

First conversation:

```text
MARA:
You closing tomorrow too?

PLAYER CHARACTER:
Depends if Hollis remembers labor laws.

MARA:
So yes.
```

Second prompt:

```text
MARA:
You hear about the lights over Blackneedle?

PLAYER CHARACTER:
Rook Signal?

MARA:
That's what people keep saying when they want to stop talking.
```

Optional player responses:

```text
- You sound worried.
- You sound bored.
- You want me to check it out?
```

Mara response to worried:

```text
MARA:
I sound observant. Worrying takes energy.
```

Mara response to bored:

```text
MARA:
I would love to be bored. Briarwake keeps making that difficult.
```

Mara response to check it out:

```text
MARA:
Absolutely not. If something is glowing in the woods at midnight, that is the woods' private business.
```

Completion:

- sets `scene01_spoke_to_mara`
- establishes Mara as practical and alert

### Task: Help The Last Customer

Availability: After two pre-Silas interactions.

Location: Store entrance, aisles, front counter.

Silas enters and shops. The player can follow, talk, or wait at the counter.

Required action:

- ring up Silas at the counter

Checkout item list:

- batteries
- bandages
- canned coffee
- road flares from behind the counter if the player has spoken to Hollis about them

Optional road flare branch:

```text
SILAS:
You still sell the little red road flares?

HOLLIS:
We sell festive tubes of liability, yes.
```

Completion:

- sets `scene01_served_silas`
- unlocks the required window check

### Task: Check The Front Windows

Availability: After Silas leaves.

Location: Front windows.

Required action:

- inspect window or talk to quiet customer

Window text:

```text
Ashbell Road is almost empty.
Past the last streetlight, Blackneedle Woods sits in a dark line.
Something pale moves behind the trees.
Not across them.
Behind them.
```

Completion:

- sets `scene01_checked_window`
- starts final normal moment
- leads to pressure drop and crash

## Optional Interactions Before The Crash

```text
The freezer buzzes like it is trying to remember a song.
```

Second interaction:

```text
You kick the lower panel twice.
The buzz stops.
Three seconds later, it comes back lower than before.
```

Hollis reaction:

```text
HOLLIS:
See? It respects confidence.
```

Mara reaction:

```text
MARA:
That machine is going to outlive all of us out of spite.
```

### Community Board

Interaction:

```text
The board is crowded with local notices, curled flyers, and one unpaid parking warning someone pinned up as a threat.
```

Selectable notices:

```text
Foundry Row Block Sale - Saturday
Vesper Clinic Blood Drive
Rook Signal Maintenance Road Closed
Calder Yard Redevelopment Meeting Postponed
Briarwake Harvest Parade Volunteers Needed
```

Each notice can have a one-line inspection.

Foundry Row:

```text
Half the town owns a folding table because of Foundry Row block sales.
```

Vesper Clinic:

```text
Vesper Clinic always needs donors. The poster has been photocopied so many times the nurse looks haunted.
```
