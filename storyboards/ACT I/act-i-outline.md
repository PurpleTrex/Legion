# Legion Storyboard - Act I

## Act Title

`Act I: The First Night`

## Location Rule

All cities, towns, streets, businesses, landmarks, agencies, and institutions in `Legion` are fictional. Real-world states, cities, counties, brands, and agencies should not be used in story scenes.

## Primary Setting

### Town

`Briarwake`

A small, isolated company town built around an old rail yard, a shuttered mineral plant, and a stretch of dense pine woods locals call `The Blackneedle`. Briarwake is ordinary enough to feel familiar, but cut off enough that the first night can spiral before outside help arrives.

### Region

`Kestrel County`

A fictional rural county with poor cell coverage, old industrial roads, scattered farms, and one main highway out. Locals are used to power issues, bad weather, and strange noises from the old plant, which gives the crash a few minutes of plausible denial.

### Key Act I Locations

- `Marlowe's Market`: The main character's workplace and opening playable area.
- `Ashbell Road`: The road outside the market, leading toward the first visible glow.
- `Rook Signal Tower`: A radio tower outside town that begins receiving strange interference.
- `Blackneedle Woods`: The forest where the crash lands.
- `Harrowgate Crossing`: A blocked rail crossing used as the first police perimeter.
- `Vesper Clinic`: A small urgent-care clinic that becomes a possible safehouse.
- `Foundry Row`: Old worker housing near the abandoned plant.
- `The Hush Drain`: A storm-drain route that bypasses the first roadblock.
- `Calder Yard`: The abandoned rail yard where the first alien fragment can be found.

## Act I Core Question

What happens to an ordinary town during the first few hours after something lands nearby and begins turning people into one shared organism?

## Act I Player Fantasy

The player is not a soldier, scientist, or chosen hero. They are a tired local worker trying to understand what happened, keep people alive, and hold onto their own identity while the town starts thinking with one mind.

## Act I Narrative Function

Act I should:

- Introduce Briarwake as a believable place before it becomes hostile.
- Establish the main character's normal life through interaction, not exposition.
- Show the crash from the ground level.
- Introduce infection symptoms gradually.
- Recruit the first party member.
- Teach exploration, visible field enemies, and turn-based combat with all combatants shown onscreen.
- Reveal the first alien fragment without fully explaining Legion.
- End with the town changed and the heroes forced to choose a safehouse.

## Main Character Placeholder

The main character does not need a final name yet. Until decided, use:

`Player Character`

Potential later naming directions:

- grounded, ordinary, local name
- gender-selectable name
- fixed protagonist name with stronger authored dialogue

## Opening Job

The recommended opening job is a late shift at `Marlowe's Market`, a small independent grocery and convenience store on the edge of Briarwake.

Reasons this works:

- It gives the player a compact tutorial space.
- It naturally includes coworkers, customers, radios, shelves, phones, windows, back rooms, and street access.
- It can hold humor, routine, local gossip, and unsettling background details.
- It places the protagonist near ordinary citizens when the crash happens.
- It gives access to early items without feeling artificial.

## Act I Scene List

### Scene 1: Closing Shift at Marlowe's Market

The player works the last minutes of a normal shift. Strange details appear in the background, but nobody understands their meaning yet.

### Scene 2: The Crash Beyond Blackneedle

The impact shakes the market, kills the lights, breaks radio signals, and paints the sky above the woods with an impossible glow.

### Scene 3: Aftershock Inside the Market

The player checks on coworkers and customers. One person speaks as if something inside them has opened.

### Scene 4: Ashbell Road

The player steps outside and sees Briarwake reacting in real time. Sirens, neighbors, panic, denial, and the first visible signs of aggression.

### Scene 5: First Hostile Encounter

An infected local attacks. Combat begins on the same screen space where the enemy was visible before contact.

### Scene 6: First Party Member

The player helps a local ally escape danger. This person joins the party for immediate survival, not because they understand the larger threat.

### Scene 7: Harrowgate Crossing

Police and town officials block the main route to Blackneedle Woods. Authority appears functional for a moment, then the infection shows through.

### Scene 8: The Hush Drain

The party takes a hidden route beneath the roadblock. The environment becomes more contaminated, wet, echoing, and biologically wrong.

### Scene 9: Calder Yard Fragment

The party finds a small piece of the crashed organism. This is the first direct alien encounter and the first hint that the crash is alive.

### Scene 10: The Name in the Static

After the fragment fight, radios, phones, or nearby infected voices briefly synchronize around one word: `Legion`.

### Scene 11: Return to Briarwake

The town is worse than before. More people are outside, some violent, some silent, some moving together without speaking.

### Scene 12: First Safehouse Choice

The party chooses where to regroup: `Vesper Clinic`, the `Marlowe's Market` back room, or a residence on `Foundry Row`.

## Act I Emotional Arc

1. **Routine:** The player is doing ordinary work.
2. **Disruption:** The crash makes the familiar world unstable.
3. **Concern:** People are hurt, confused, and frightened.
4. **Wrongness:** Some people react in ways that are not humanly normal.
5. **Threat:** The first infected person attacks.
6. **Connection:** The first party member joins.
7. **Distrust:** Local authority cannot be relied on.
8. **Discovery:** The party sees alien biology directly.
9. **Revelation:** The name `Legion` enters the story.
10. **Commitment:** The player can no longer return to normal life.

## Infection Progression in Act I

### Stage 0: Unexposed

Normal human behavior.

### Stage 1: Emotional Amplification

The person becomes unusually angry, afraid, euphoric, obsessive, or calm.

### Stage 2: Phrase Echo

The person repeats phrases they should not know, often spoken by other infected people elsewhere.

### Stage 3: Group Pull

The person is drawn toward the crash site, alien fragments, or other infected people.

### Stage 4: Defensive Violence

The person attacks anyone who interferes with the spread or tries to separate infected people from the group.

Act I should mostly show stages 1 through 3. Stage 4 appears in combat encounters, but full transformation should wait until later acts.

## Combat Presentation Notes

- Enemies are visible on the field before combat starts.
- Combat does not cut to an abstract battle void.
- The screen can frame the current area as the combat arena.
- Player party sprites stand on one side of the visible space.
- Enemy sprites stand on the other side.
- Characters step forward, animate, then return to position.
- Status effects should have visible sprite behavior: shaking, doubled outlines, synchronized idle frames, or momentary loss of control.

## Act I Ending State

By the end of Act I:

- The crash is confirmed to be alive.
- The town is partially compromised.
- The first party member has joined.
- The name `Legion` has been heard.
- The player has seen that the organism uses people, emotion, and proximity.
- The heroes have a temporary safehouse but no full explanation.
- The next objective is to find someone who can understand the signal, biology, or townwide pattern.

## Open Decisions

- Final name, age, and personality of the main character.
- Whether the first party member is a coworker, customer, neighbor, or old friend.
- Whether `Marlowe's Market` is owned by someone important to the story.
- Whether the first safehouse is chosen by the player or fixed by the story.
- Whether the crash itself is visible in Act I or only its fragments are seen.
